# Our Services

This is our Description of Services page and Costs Associated.

All calls, and any visit thereafter will include a $85.00 Service Fee, other than Quoted work.

For all hourly calls, the clock starts when we show up, and ends when we put the last tool away.

We charge in 1 hour increments, and if you go any time into an additional hour, you will be charged for it.

All costs on this page do not include tax of 8.3% that will be assessed to all Parts and Service, all costs including tax are due at time of service.

Drain / Sewer Cleaning and Unclogging

This is charged Hourly at $125.00 per hour for Residential work and $150.00 per hour for Commercial work. An additional fee of $150.00 is applicable if it is decided, by the Technician, that a Mainline Sewer Machine is required. A Sewer Inspection may be necessary in order to give an answer to the reason behind clog and the cost for that is listed next.

All Calls include $85.00 Service Fee.
